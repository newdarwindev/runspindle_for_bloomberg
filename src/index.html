01 / Method

A run should leave a record.

Each scenario names a market observation, a bounded next step, and a checkable result. A pause or refusal is recorded as its own outcome; it is not counted as completed work.

01

Observe

Capture the exact security, benchmark, event, date and source conditions required by the task.

02

Decide or pause

Use a stated rule and an operator gate when identity, access, data rights or real-world effects are uncertain.

03

Verify

Match the authorized result to a receipt, source record or later market mark. Keep the evidence with the decision.
